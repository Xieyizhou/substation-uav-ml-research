# v0.1 LiDAR Validation — 2026-07-28

## Scope

This run validates two simulation-only gates:

1. a continuous 600-second Gazebo 2D LiDAR capture; and
2. live-LiDAR closed-loop PX4 flights to multiple targets in the
   `substation_complex_v1` and `substation_extreme_v1` worlds.

The vehicle was `x500_research`. Flight perception used
`gazebo_lidar_2d`, the geometric risk model, a rolling local costmap, and the
`slow_down` safety response. It did not use the map-oracle detector as its
perception source.

Aggregate result: 6/6 missions completed, 6/6 landings confirmed, zero raw
physical collisions, zero inflated safety-buffer entries, and zero reported
LiDAR drops during flight.

Across both maps, the six runs total 1,288.365 seconds of flight with a
214.727-second mean. The worst observed in-flight P95 frame age was 31.638 ms,
the worst P95 geometric inference latency was 3.106 ms, and the smallest
forward LiDAR return was 1.940 m.

The offline map-aware trajectory checker reported near-boundary clearances
between 0.76 m and 0.97 m. These are warnings, not collision or buffer-entry
events, and should be retained when comparing later models.

## Evidence Boundaries

- These are Gazebo/PX4 SITL results, not real-airframe results.
- The risk model is geometric, not ML.
- Six runs across two maps and three target types are route-diversity evidence,
  not a statistical benchmark.
- Raw scans, CSV telemetry, PX4 ULog files, and generated plots remain local
  and outside Git. Only this summary and its machine-readable companion are
  tracked.
